# Convertra macOS — Installation Guide

## System Requirements
- macOS 13.0 (Ventura) or later.
- Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) or Intel processor.
- ~15 MB of free disk space.

## Installation Steps
1. Download the `Convertra.zip` archive from our website.
2. Locate the downloaded file in your **Downloads** folder.
3. Double-click `Convertra.zip` to extract it.
4. Drag and drop the extracted **Convertra.app** into your **Applications** folder.
5. Launch Convertra from your Applications folder or Launchpad.

## Troubleshooting
**"Convertra.app can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software"**
- Open **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security**.
- Scroll down to the **Security** section.
- Click **Open Anyway** next to the warning about Convertra.
- Click **Open** on the prompt that appears.
